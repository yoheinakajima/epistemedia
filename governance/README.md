# Governance Records

Governance proposals, evaluator reports, canary results, promotion decisions, rejections, quarantines, forks, and forward-only disablements are append-only accepted records.

A governance result identifies the accepted base, candidate SHA, affected authority layers, policies, evaluator lineages, historical replay corpus, outcome diffs, precommitted promotion predicates, canary bounds, decision reason codes, and limitations.

No proposal or evaluator output has authority merely because it appears in this directory. Admission follows the accepted constitution and trusted integration process.
