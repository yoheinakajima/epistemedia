## Method

2. Prefer primary research, official records, author or institutional manuscripts, registered
   data/code, and authoritative examination statistics.
3. Record exact work and edition identity, URLs, identifiers, retrieval date, media type, access,
   license or quotation treatment, and unresolved artifacts.
4. Keep circulated propositions separate from tested or reported propositions.
5. Map data, author, method, material, citation, comparison-class, and derivation dependence
   without converting expected topology into an evidentiary count.
6. Search for corrections, replies, reanalyses, supersession, and material counterevidence.
7. End each packet with a provisional readiness disposition and bounded cost estimate.
8. Obtain fresh-clone independent review before protected merge.
